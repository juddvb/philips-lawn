- **Audio fade out over the last 1.0 s and fade picture to black** over the same second, so the clip does not end mid-note. Reel 4 gets 1.5 s.
- Reframe from 16:9: center-crop for wide shots, but keep text cards, the logo and the "26" counter fully inside the safe area (10 % from top, 20 % from bottom are covered by app UI). Pan the crop to follow faces or vans when the subject is off-center.

- Cover frame: Reel 1 the building in flames; Reel 2 the number 26 on screen; Reel 3 the best van exterior; Reel 4 the "Someday is a decision" card.
